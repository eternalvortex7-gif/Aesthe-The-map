About aesthe.

aesthe is a free map built on open data. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

The small 3D cars, buses, motorbikes, bicycles and walkers, the metro and railway trains, the barricades and the gentle camera sway are decoration (switch them all off with Motion). They use no extra data and are not live: the trains run along the real tracks on the map but follow no timetable, and the cars, buses and bikes are not real traffic (they are drawn a little larger than real when you are zoomed out, so they can be seen).

A barricade on a road that the map marks as under construction comes from map data. The other barricades, with cones and a blinking light, are staged now and then for show and do not mean a road is really closed. Never use aesthe to decide whether a road or a train is running.

What leaves your device

  • The map area you view goes to the map tile server (OpenFreeMap).
  • What you type in the search box and the rough centre of the map go to the Photon search service. If you press Enter, the same search also goes to Nominatim (OpenStreetMap) and to Overpass.
  • Categories and nearby places send the map area to Overpass servers.
  • Directions send the start and end points to the OSRM routing service.
  • Satellite view: only while it is on, the map tiles you look at are fetched from the satellite picture provider chosen in this site’s settings, which can see which map areas you view, as the normal tile server does. By default that is EOX (Austria), whose free pictures are about 10 m per pixel and so turn blurry when zoomed in close; if the site owner added an Esri or MapTiler key, sharp pictures from that provider are used instead (and the free ones only if it cannot be reached). Satellite pictures are not saved for offline use.
  • Weather: the position of the place you open (or, only after you tap “Use my location” on the weather card, where you are), rounded to about 1 km, goes to Open-Meteo.com.
  • Photos: when you mark a place, its name and position go to Wikimedia Commons, Wikipedia and Wikidata (and to Mapillary if you add a token).
  • Your location is read only when you tap the location button or “Use my location” on the weather card, ask for something “near me”, or start directions. If you chose the weather card once, it reads your location again on later visits, but only while your browser still allows location for this site. It is not stored.
  • Saved places, recent searches and photos you add stay on this device.

If this site runs its own aesthe server, those requests pass through it first, and it may keep ordinary logs for a short time to stop abuse. Details: Privacy & credits.

Safety

Directions come from community map data and can be wrong or out of date. Follow road signs and local laws, and do not use your phone while driving.
