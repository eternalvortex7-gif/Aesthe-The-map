Privacy & credits

Last updated: 14 October 2026

In short: aesthe has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies. Your saved places, recent searches and photos stay on your device. To find places, routes and photos, the app has to send what you search for and the map area you are viewing to the map services listed below.

What aesthe uses

  • Your location, only if you allow it in the welcome message or tap the location button. It is used on your device to centre the map. It is sent to a service only when you ask for something that needs it (places near you, directions, or the weather where you are, which is rounded to about 1 km first). If you tap "Use my location" on the weather card, aesthe remembers only that you chose it, and asks the browser for your location again on later visits.
  • What you search for and the area of the map you are looking at. These are sent to the search and map-data services to get results.
  • Photos you add to a place. They are resized and stored only in your browser. They are never uploaded.

Stored on your device

Saved places, recent searches, your settings (map style, language, voice, photos on/off), photos you added, map files you saved for offline use, and, if you choose to enter one, your optional Mapillary token. This lives in your browser's storage. You can remove all of it at any time with About → Clear my data, or by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Directions and safety

Routes and directions come from community map data and may be wrong or out of date. Check road signs and local rules. Do not use your phone while driving. aesthe is provided as is, without any guarantee of accuracy or availability.

Photos

Photos hung above a marked place come from the sources shown under each photo. Wikimedia Commons photos are shared under free licences (such as CC BY-SA); the photographer and licence are shown under the photo and when you open it. A photo marked "nearby" shows something close to the place, not necessarily the place itself.

Children

aesthe is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
